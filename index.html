Take-home pay targets

Why try 70%? It’s a common starting point because some costs, like travel to work, may fall when you retire. If most of your pay goes towards essentials or you’re working fewer hours, you may need closer to all of your take-home pay, or more.

How we estimate tax

Uses 2026–27 Australian resident tax rates and the low income tax offset. The Medicare levy uses the 2% rate and 2025–26 single thresholds. We estimate tax for each person separately. The estimate excludes HELP repayments, the Medicare levy surcharge and other offsets or adjustments.
