How we estimate tax

Uses 2026–27 Australian resident income tax rates and the low income tax offset. The Medicare levy uses the 2% rate and 2025–26 single thresholds. The estimate assumes your salary is your taxable income and excludes HELP repayments, the Medicare levy surcharge and other offsets or adjustments. For a more precise starting point, enter your after-tax pay.

Why start at 70%? It’s a common rule of thumb because some costs, like travel to work, may fall when you retire. Try a few percentages to see what would cover your spending.

Percentage of take-home pay
When might I need more or less?

If most of your pay goes towards essentials, you may need close to 100% of it. If you’ve recently cut back your hours, even all of your current pay may fall short of what you want to spend.

If your hours vary or you work casually, try the amount you usually take home over a full year. If you expect to have fewer bills in retirement, a lower percentage might suit you.
